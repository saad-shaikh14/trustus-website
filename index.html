At TRUSTUS Care, we provide high-quality Supported Living, Domiciliary Care, and Complex Care services for adults with Learning Disabilities, Autism, Mental Health needs, and other complex support requirements. Why Choose Us

Our carers are fully trained, DBS-checked and passionate about making a genuine difference in people's lives.

Every individual receives a support plan tailored around their goals, preferences and needs — built with the individual, not just for them.

We understand needs can arise quickly. We respond fast, set up care efficiently and remain flexible as circumstances change.

We work closely with families, local authorities, commissioners and healthcare professionals for the best possible outcomes.

How It Works

Call us or send a message. We'll listen carefully and answer any questions — no obligation, no sales pitch.

A care manager visits to meet the individual and understand what they actually need. We build a plan with you, not just for you.

We match the right support worker, start at a comfortable pace and check in regularly during the first weeks to make sure everything is right.

TRUSTUS Care is committed to delivering compassionate, reliable, and person-centred care that promotes independence, dignity, and quality of life for every individual we support.
