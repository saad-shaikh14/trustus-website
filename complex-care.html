Monica Supported Living

High-quality supported accommodation for 6 residents — empowering individuals to live fulfilling, independent lives with professional support tailored to their personal needs and goals.

About Monica

Supported Independent Living

Monica Supported Living provides a purpose-adapted home for 6 residents, with on-site 24-hour support. We focus on empowering individuals to build confidence, develop life skills and take control of their own lives.

Each resident benefits from an individual tenancy agreement, a personalised support plan and a dedicated keyworker who they can build a genuine relationship with over time.

  • 6-room property with private rooms and communal spaces
  • 24-hour on-site support from trained specialists
  • Person-centred support plans reviewed regularly
  • Strong focus on community participation and social inclusion
Who We Support

Adults With a Range of Needs

Monica Supported Living supports adults across different levels of complexity — all with the same commitment to dignity, choice and positive outcomes.

Learning Disabilities

Personalised support helping individuals build everyday skills and live more independently.

Autism Spectrum

Consistent, structured environments designed around sensory needs and individual communication preferences.

Mental Health Needs

Stable, therapeutic home environments with compassionate staff focused on recovery and wellbeing.

Challenging Behaviour

Positive Behaviour Support approaches delivered by experienced, PBS-trained staff.

Emotional & Behavioural Difficulties

Trauma-informed support that understands the root causes of behaviour and responds with empathy.

Complex Support Needs

Skilled teams supporting individuals with multiple, overlapping and complex requirements.

Day-to-Day Life

What Monica Supported Living Includes

Support at Monica is built around each individual’s goals, preferences and daily rhythm. Staff work alongside residents — not for them — building confidence and independence step by step.

  • Personal care and daily living support
  • Meal planning, cooking and nutrition guidance
  • Medication management and healthcare appointments
  • Community access, social activities and outings
  • Support with education, employment and personal development
  • Emotional wellbeing and mental health support

Referrals

How Referrals Work

We make the referral process straightforward and supportive from start to finish.

1

Get in Touch

Call or email us. We’ll listen to the individual’s situation, answer questions and confirm whether Monica is a good fit.

2

Assessment & Matching

We carry out a thorough needs assessment with the individual, family and placing authority to match the right home and support level.

3

Move In & Settle

We support the transition carefully — with introductory visits, a gradual move-in and a dedicated keyworker from day one.
