Complex Care

Specialist Care for Complex Needs

We provide specialist care and support for individuals with complex health and behavioural needs. Our experienced staff are trained to support individuals across a wide range of conditions and circumstances.

  • Autism spectrum conditions
  • Mental health needs
  • Behaviours of concern
  • Learning disabilities
  • Physical disabilities
  • Acquired brain injuries
  • PEG feeding support
  • Mobility and moving & handling support
  • High-dependency care needs

All care plans are developed in partnership with healthcare professionals and tailored to individual clinical and personal requirements.

How We Help

How TRUSTUS Care Can Help

Our experienced and highly trained support teams provide compassionate, specialist care tailored to each individual's unique circumstances and goals. We focus on promoting independence, improving quality of life and helping individuals remain connected to their communities.

Our Complex Care Services Include

  • 24-hour support and waking night care
  • Personal care and daily living assistance
  • Medication administration and monitoring
  • Behavioural support and structured routines
  • Community access and social inclusion
  • Emotional wellbeing and mental health support
  • Support with appointments, education and employment goals
  • Assistance with mobility, nutrition and health management
  • Hospital discharge and transition support
  • Family liaison and collaborative care planning

At TRUSTUS Care, we understand that complex care requires consistency, trust and specialist knowledge. Our goal is to empower every individual to live a safer, healthier and more fulfilling life while receiving the right support in the least restrictive and most supportive environment possible.
