About Stanmore

Empowering Independent Living

Stanmore Supported Living provides a comfortable, purpose-adapted home with round-the-clock specialist support. Our focus is on helping residents build independence, develop life skills and live life on their own terms.

Each resident has their own tenancy agreement, their own space and a dedicated support team — with the freedom to make their own choices and the support to make them safely.

  • 4-room property in the Stanmore area, London
  • 24-hour on-site support from trained staff
  • Individual support plans built around personal goals
  • Community integration and social activities
Who We Support

Specialist Support for Complex Needs

Our Stanmore homes are designed for adults with a range of needs — each supported by a bespoke, person-centred plan.

Learning Disabilities

Tailored support to build everyday skills, independence and confidence.

Autism Spectrum

Structured, sensory-aware environments adapted to each individual's specific needs and routines.

Mental Health Needs

Compassionate, recovery-focused support promoting emotional wellbeing and stability.

Positive Behaviour Support

Staff trained in PBS frameworks to understand and respond to behaviours with dignity.

Transition from Hospital

Smooth, carefully planned moves from hospital or residential placements into supported living.

Complex Support Needs

Experienced teams supporting individuals with multiple and complex requirements.

Day-to-Day Life

What Our Support Includes

Support at Stanmore is always led by what the individual wants from their life. Our staff work alongside residents — encouraging choice, growth and independence at every stage.

  • Personal care and daily living support
  • Meal planning, cooking and nutrition
  • Community outings and social activities
  • Support with education, employment and volunteering
  • Medication management and healthcare appointments
  • Family contact and relationship support
  • Physical exercise and wellbeing activities
  • Support with budgeting and daily finances
Referrals

How Referrals Work

We make the referral process as simple and supportive as possible.

1

Get in Touch

Contact us by phone or through our online form. We’ll listen carefully, answer your questions and discuss what you’re looking for — with no pressure or obligation.

2

Needs Assessment

We carry out a thorough assessment with the individual, their family and any relevant professionals to identify the most suitable property and design a personalised support plan.

3

Move In & Settle

We plan the move carefully, introduce the support team beforehand and stay closely involved in the settling-in period to ensure the transition is positive and well-supported.
