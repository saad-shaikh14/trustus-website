Supported Living

Empowering Independence Within a Supportive Home

We provide tailored supported living services for adults who require varying levels of support while maintaining their independence within our homes or shared living environments.

Our support is person-centred, flexible, and built around each individual's goals, preferences, and personal aspirations.

  • Daily living support & medication assistance
  • Emotional wellbeing & community access support
  • Budgeting, tenancy, and household support
  • Meal preparation & positive behaviour support
  • Appointment & activity support
  • 24/7 on-site staff availability

Referrals

How Referrals Work

We welcome referrals from local authorities, social workers, NHS teams, families, and healthcare professionals. Our team works quickly and collaboratively to ensure the right placement.

1

Get in Touch

Contact our referrals team by phone or email. We'll discuss the individual's needs and check for availability.

2

Assessment & Matching

We carry out a thorough assessment to ensure a suitable placement match and develop a tailored support plan.

3

Move In & Settle

We support a smooth transition into the property, with ongoing reviews to ensure the placement continues to work well.
