About TRUSTUS Care

A specialist care provider dedicated to empowering adults to live with dignity, independence, and confidence.

About Us

Who We Are

TRUSTUS Care is a specialist care provider dedicated to supporting adults with Learning Disabilities, Autism, Mental Health conditions, and Complex Needs across community-based settings.

We believe that every individual deserves the opportunity to live with dignity, independence, and confidence within their own home and community. Our services are designed to provide personalised support that promotes wellbeing, develops life skills, and encourages meaningful participation in everyday life.

Our experienced and compassionate team works closely with individuals, families, social workers, healthcare professionals, and local authorities to create safe, structured, and outcome-focused support plans tailored to each person's needs.

Whether providing support at home, within supported living environments, or through specialist complex care packages, our focus remains the same: To deliver care with compassion, build trust through consistency, and promote independence through empowerment.

Our Mission

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide safe, compassionate, and person-centred care that empowers individuals to live fulfilling and independent lives. We are committed to:

  • Promoting dignity, respect, and equality
  • Encouraging independence and personal growth
  • Delivering high-quality, outcome-focused support
  • Creating safe and supportive environments
  • Building trusted relationships with individuals, families, and professionals
  • Supporting people to actively participate within their communities

At TRUSTUS Care, we do not believe in "one-size-fits-all" care. Every support package is tailored around the individual's goals, strengths, preferences, and aspirations.

Who We Support

Supporting Adults Aged 18+

TRUSTUS Care supports adults with a wide range of support needs. We recognise that every individual is unique — our support is designed around each person's abilities, preferences, communication styles, cultural values, and long-term goals.

Person-centred support enabling individuals to develop skills and live independently.

Structured, consistent support tailored to the unique needs and communication styles of each individual.

Compassionate support promoting positive mental wellbeing, recovery, and community participation.

Specialist support packages for individuals with complex and high-dependency care requirements.

Positive behaviour support strategies that promote wellbeing and reduce restrictive practices.

Safe, supported transitions from hospital into community settings — including acquired brain injuries and dual diagnosis.
