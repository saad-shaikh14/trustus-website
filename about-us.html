Our Story

About TRUSTUS Group

TRUSTUS Group is a CQC-registered adult social care organisation operating three specialist services across the UK — BrightLife Home Care, Stanmore Supported Living and Monica Supported Living.

We believe every person, regardless of their needs, deserves compassionate, professional support that promotes independence, choice and dignity. Our experienced teams work closely with individuals, families, local authorities and healthcare professionals to deliver outstanding outcomes.

From flexible home care to 24-hour supported living, we are committed to meeting people where they are and helping them live the lives they choose.
