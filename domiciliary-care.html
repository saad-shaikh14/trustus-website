About BrightLife

What is BrightLife Home Care?

BrightLife Home Care is TRUSTUS Group’s professional home care service, delivering person-centred support to adults across the UK. Our trained carers work flexibly around each individual’s routine, preferences and goals.

Whether you need a few hours of support each week or round-the-clock live-in care, we design a package that fits — and we adapt it as your needs change.

  • Flexible care hours — from a few visits to full live-in care
  • Support tailored to the individual's goals and routines
  • Consistent carers who build trusted relationships
  • Available 24/7, including nights, weekends and bank holidays
Who We Support

Supporting Adults Across a Range of Needs

Our staff are trained to support people through different life stages and levels of complexity — so families don’t need to switch providers as things change.

Elderly Care

Companionship, personal care and daily living support — helping people stay safe and comfortable at home.

Learning Disabilities

Skill-building, community access and daily support for adults living full, independent lives.

Autism Support

Sensory-aware, structured support with consistent routines and communication approaches that respect each person.

Mental Health

Compassionate, recovery-focused support for adults with enduring mental health conditions.

Emotional & Behavioural Difficulties

Positive Behaviour Support delivered by staff trained to understand the individual behind the behaviour.

Complex Care

Specialist support for individuals with complex physical and clinical needs — including reablement and complex care packages.

Our Services

What BrightLife Home Care Includes

We provide a full range of home care services designed around each individual. Our flexible packages can include any combination of the following:

  • Personal care and hygiene support
  • Medication administration and management
  • Companionship and emotional support
  • Meal preparation and nutrition support
  • Domestic support and housekeeping
  • Shopping and errands assistance
  • Community access and social activities
  • Mental health and wellbeing support
  • Autism and learning disability support
  • Overnight and waking night care
  • Live-in care
  • Respite care for family carers
Getting Started

How to Arrange Care

Arranging care is straightforward. Our team will guide you every step of the way.

1

Call or Message Us

Get in touch and tell us about the individual. We’ll listen and answer any questions — no obligation.

2

Free Care Assessment

A care coordinator visits to understand the individual’s needs, preferences and goals. We then design a tailored care package together.

3

Care Begins

We match a consistent carer, start at a comfortable pace and check in regularly to make sure everything is working well.
