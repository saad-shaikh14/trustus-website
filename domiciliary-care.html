Domiciliary / Home Care

TRUSTUS Care's professional domiciliary / home care service delivers compassionate, person-centred support to adults across the UK. Our trained carers provide flexible assistance tailored around each individual's lifestyle, preferences, routines and personal goals.

Whether you require a few hours of support each week, daily visits, overnight assistance or full live-in care, we create a personalised care package that evolves alongside your changing needs.

Services include:

  • Personal care
  • Medication support
  • Meal preparation
  • Domestic assistance
  • Companionship
  • Welfare checks
  • Shopping and community support
  • Overnight and live-in care
Domiciliary / Home Care

Our domiciliary care services enable individuals to remain safe, comfortable, and independent within their own homes while receiving the support they need.

Flexible care arrangements — from short daily visits to full live-in care
Personalised support tailored to individual needs, routines and independence goals
Consistent carers who build trusted, meaningful relationships
Support with personal care, medication, meals, appointments and companionship
Available 24/7, including evenings, nights, weekends and bank holidays
Promoting dignity, wellbeing, choice and independent living at home
Who We Support

Our domiciliary care team is experienced in supporting individuals with a wide variety of care needs — all within the comfort and familiarity of their own home.

Person-centred home support enabling individuals with learning disabilities to live independently.

Consistent, structured support delivered sensitively around individual routines and sensory needs.

Compassionate support promoting positive mental wellbeing and everyday independence at home.

Collaborative care with families, GPs, social workers and other healthcare professionals.

Empowerment and well-being strategies delivered in the home environment to promote positive outcomes.

Specialist domiciliary support for individuals with complex health and care requirements.

Getting Started

We make it simple and stress-free to arrange the right care package — at a pace that suits you.

1

Call us or send a message. We'll listen carefully, answer your questions and arrange a free, no-obligation care assessment.

2

A care manager visits to understand exactly what is needed. We create a personalised care plan with you — not just for you.

3

We match the right carer, begin at a comfortable pace and check in regularly to make sure everything is working perfectly.

TRUSTUS Care is committed to delivering compassionate, reliable, and person-centred care that promotes independence, dignity, and quality of life for every individual we support.
