Why Join Us

More Than Just a Job

Working in care is one of the most rewarding careers you can have. At TRUSTUS Group, you’ll be part of a supportive team that genuinely makes a difference — every single day.

  • Competitive pay and benefits package
  • Fully funded training and qualifications
  • Real career progression pathways
  • Flexible working patterns to suit your life
  • Supportive management and regular supervision
  • Pension scheme and employee wellbeing support

What We Offer

Benefits of Working With Us

Competitive Pay

Fair, competitive rates of pay reviewed regularly, with additional payments for nights and weekends.

Funded Training

All training is fully funded — from induction to specialist qualifications. We invest in your development.

Career Progression

Clear pathways from support worker to senior, team leader and beyond. We promote from within wherever possible.

Flexible Hours

Full-time, part-time and flexible shift patterns available — we work around your life, not the other way around.

Wellbeing Support

Access to our employee assistance programme, mental health support and a genuinely caring management team.

Supportive Team

You’re never alone. Our managers are approachable, responsive and genuinely invested in your success.
